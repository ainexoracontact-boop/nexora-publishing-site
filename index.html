C2L AI is a desktop content-creation and publishing workflow for short educational videos about artificial intelligence, AI tools, and their real-world impact.

What the application does

The workflow helps prepare scripts, narration, captions, and short-form videos, and manages publishing preparation for supported platforms, including YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

Platform account connections use the platforms’ official authorization mechanisms. Posting features depend on the permissions and approvals granted by each platform.

Account authorization and control

The application is intended for use with accounts explicitly authorized by their owners. Publishing integrations may remain disabled until setup and approval requirements are met.
