Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

This policy describes the C2L AI desktop content-creation and publishing workflow and this informational website. C2L AI can connect to third-party services, including TikTok and YouTube, when the account owner explicitly authorizes access.

2. Information used by integrations

Depending on the permissions granted, platform integrations may access basic account information, authorization credentials, content selected for upload, and publishing status information. Requested permissions may include TikTok’s user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.

3. Why information is used

Account information is used to identify an authorized connection. Authorization credentials are used to request permitted API actions. Video files and metadata are used to prepare uploads or publications selected through the workflow. Status data supports tracking and prevention of duplicate posts.

4. Storage and security

The desktop workflow is designed to use local processing and protected credential storage. Security and retention depend on the actual deployment and configuration. Do not place access tokens, client secrets, or passwords in public repositories or support messages.

5. Third-party processing

When an authorized account is connected or content is submitted, the relevant platform processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. See TikTok’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices

You can choose whether to authorize a supported platform integration. You may revoke an integration through the relevant platform’s account settings. Revocation may prevent further API actions but does not necessarily remove content already published on that platform.

7. Requests and contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information controlled through C2L AI, contact ai.nexora.contact@gmail.com. Requests involving information held by TikTok or Google may also need to be addressed to those providers.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated when the application, data practices, or platform requirements change. The latest version will appear here.
