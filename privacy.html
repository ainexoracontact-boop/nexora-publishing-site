Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

Nexora Publishing is a locally operated content management and publishing application.

2. Information Accessed

With user authorization, Nexora Publishing may access basic TikTok account information and the permissions necessary to publish video content.

3. Use of Information

Information is used solely for account authentication, content management and user-authorized publishing operations.

4. Data Storage

The application operates locally. Account credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored locally and are not sold to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Publishing operations may involve TikTok APIs and are subject to TikTok's applicable policies.

6. User Control

Users can revoke Nexora Publishing's access through their TikTok account settings.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator using the contact information provided through the official Nexora Publishing channels.
